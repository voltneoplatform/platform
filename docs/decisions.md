# Technical decisions

Business decisions live in the Foundation's Decision Log (D1–D52). This file
records technical choices made while building, so they are not changed silently.
Status labels follow the project convention: CONFIRMED (founder decided),
PROPOSED (Claude recommends), ASSUMED (working assumption).

## Milestone M3 (collections)

| #   | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Why                                                                                | Status                                                         |
| --- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| T49 | The daily check (00:05 IST) and reminders and notices (09:00 IST) run on BullMQ and Redis, switched on per process with `JOBS_ENABLED`. Each job records each business date it has done (`job_runs`), so a date never runs twice; up to 7 missed days are caught up, judged against payments as they stand now                                                 | Spec section 6 and the confirmed stack (T1). Messages go at 09:00, not at midnight | CONFIRMED (founder, 24 Oct 2026), incl. 7-day catch-up         |
| T50 | A late spell is stored on the agreement from the oldest missed due date and ends when every instalment that has fallen due and every late fee is paid. The day's late fee is added by the 00:05 run, linked to the oldest overdue instalment; no fee is charged on unpaid late fees alone                                                                      | D38 as changed on 23 Oct 2026                                                      | PROPOSED                                                       |
| T51 | Immobilisation needs two people: the queue shows the agreement as due, one staff member asks (from day 5, the final-notice day), a different operations manager or super admin approves (from day 6). The spec said the system creates the request; with a system request only one person would be involved                                                    | D19 two-person rule                                                                | CONFIRMED (founder, 24 Oct 2026); replaces spec rule 8 wording |
| T52 | Disable and restore are done in the GPS supplier portal and recorded with the portal reference, who did it and when. A disable needs an approved request and a confirmation that the portal showed the vehicle stationary (database check). The battery stays "installed" while disabled                                                                       | D23, Foundation safety rules                                                       | PROPOSED                                                       |
| T53 | Restore needs no approval: paying everything overdue creates a restore task at the top of the queue. Switching back on without payment is allowed only with a written reason (interim emergency override, D9) and the agreement stays overdue                                                                                                                  | Restore should be fast, including at night                                         | CONFIRMED (founder, 24 Oct 2026)                               |
| T54 | Recovery: requested from 30 days after the disable, approved by a second person, then recorded by staff with recovery rights (the agreement's battery, place, condition, optional photos). The battery goes to recovered custody; unpaid instalments and late fees are written off on the agreement (no ledger entry: no money moves) and the agreement closes | D39, D44, D45                                                                      | PROPOSED; write-off accounting REQUIRES VALIDATION (CA)        |
| T55 | Paying everything overdue cancels any open immobilisation or recovery request automatically, recorded as a system action                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Avoids disabling a driver who has paid                                             | PROPOSED                                                       |
| T56 | Reminders, final notice, "battery disabled" and "switched back on" messages go by SMS in the driver's language, each at most once (`collection_notices`). WhatsApp and the voice final notice wait for providers. Wording REQUIRES VALIDATION (lawyer, native speakers)                                                                                        | D41, D33; no SMS, WhatsApp or IVR provider yet                                     | PROPOSED                                                       |
| T57 | Tests use a settable clock; the business logic reads "today" from it                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Lets tests walk day 1 to day 36                                                    | PROPOSED                                                       |
| T58 | The "In grace" state is labelled "Overdue": it covers grace days 1-5 and day 6 onwards until the battery is disabled; the collections stage shows the detail                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Avoids calling day 20 "grace"                                                      | PROPOSED                                                       |
